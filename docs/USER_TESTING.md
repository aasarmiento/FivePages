# User testing with two classmates

We want to see if someone who has never used FivePages can get through it without us explaining anything. The point is to find what is confusing, fix it, and write down what changed.

> This file is a script and a results sheet. Write only what really happened in the sessions.

## Before you start

- Install the latest build on one phone, with no books added (fresh install).
- Pick two classmates who were not on the team and have not seen the app.
- Ask permission to take notes. Do not record faces.
- Sit next to them but do not help. If they ask "what do I press?", say "what would you try?".
- Time how long each task takes.

## What to say

> "This is a reading tracker. I am testing the app, not you. If something is confusing, that is our mistake. Please say out loud what you are thinking while you use it."

## Tasks

| # | Task | Done when | What to watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up the app and get to the book list | They are on My books | Do they understand onboarding? Do they skip or read it? |
| 2 | Add a book you are reading, with a finish date | The book shows in the list | Do they understand the daily goal? Do they make the title error appear? |
| 3 | Start a reading session and log some pages | Nice one! screen shows | Do they find Start session? Do they understand the timer? |
| 4 | Change the book title, then see what you read before | Title changed and Session history opened | Do they find Book options? Do they know where Done takes them? |
| 5 | Find your streak, then open the Preview of "Missed yesterday" | Missed yesterday screen shows | Can they find Progress? Do they understand what a freeze does? |

## Questions after the tasks

Ask each, then write their answer in their own words.

1. What was the easiest part?
2. What was the most confusing part?
3. Was there a moment you did not know what to press?
4. In your own words, what does the streak freeze do?
5. Would you use this for a real book? Why or why not?
6. Rate the app from 1 (hard) to 5 (easy): ____

### Maria Garcia

1. What was the easiest part?  
   Adding the book once she saw the finish date was required.

2. What was the most confusing part?  
   The repeated plus taps for logging pages and finding the edit option at first.

3. Was there a moment you did not know what to press?  
   Only briefly when looking for how to edit the book title.

4. In your own words, what does the streak freeze do?  
   It lets you keep the streak if you miss a day. The screen was slightly unclear but she understood the idea.

5. Would you use this for a real book? Why or why not?  
   Yes, she said she would try it because the app is simple, though page logging could get annoying on longer books.

6. Rate the app from 1 (hard) to 5 (easy): 4

### Luis Santos

1. What was the easiest part?  
   The overall flow once he got past the first screens.

2. What was the most confusing part?  
   Logging pages one by one and the Done button returning to the list.

3. Was there a moment you did not know what to press?  
   Just a second while looking for the book options icon.

4. In your own words, what does the streak freeze do?  
   It protects the streak when you miss a day. The wording on the screen was a little confusing but the meaning was clear enough.

5. Would you use this for a real book? Why or why not?  
   Yes, especially if typing the page count gets added later.

6. Rate the app from 1 (hard) to 5 (easy): 4
